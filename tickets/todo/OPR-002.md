## Done when
The template carries the fixes proven on the running instance, rather than
drifting behind it.

## Why
This repo was generalised from `org-pr-review` at a point in time. That
instance keeps being fixed; this one does not. The drift starts at zero and
grows silently, and the first person to notice will be someone who deployed
the template and hit a bug already fixed upstream.

The specific things expected to land there first: the GitHub App migration
(which also removes this repo's headline known limitation), the empty
review-body fix, and delta reviews.

## Notes
Worth deciding how the two relate long-term. Options: keep porting by hand;
make the private instance a fork of this one and merge downstream; or move
development here and have the instance consume it. The third is cleanest and
the least likely, since the instance is where real reviews actually happen.
